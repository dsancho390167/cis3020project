Welcome to the Electricity Cost Analyzer!

This tool helps homeowners estimate electricity cost by appliance. You enter your electricity rate, add appliances with watts and daily hours, and the site estimates kWh usage and monthly cost. The goal is to make it obvious what’s driving your bill so you can make better choices

Get Started

To start, go to Step 1: Rate and enter your $/kWh. Then you’ll add appliances, view your dashboard totals, and later compare scenarios and tips.
