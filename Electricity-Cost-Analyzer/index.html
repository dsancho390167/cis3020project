Project Overview

What this website is trying to achieve

The Electricity Cost Analyzer is designed to help homeowners track electricity usage as accurately as possible by focusing on the appliances most likely to drive the majority of the bill. Users will enter their electric rate, search for appliances by brand and model, review auto-filled technical details when available, and add real-life usage habits to estimate monthly energy cost.

The long-term goal is to combine convenience, better accuracy, and a professional user experience in one clean tool.
