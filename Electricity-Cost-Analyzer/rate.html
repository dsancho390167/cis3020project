Electricity Cost Input

Set your cost per kilowatt-hour

Your electric bill usually lists a cost per kWh. This page saves that number so the rest of the app can estimate appliance costs more accurately.

Example: enter 0.14 if your rate is 14 cents per kWh.

Choose dollars if entering 0.14. Choose cents if entering 14.

Preview

Go to Appliances

Why this matters

Every appliance estimate depends on the electricity rate. Once this is saved, the app can turn energy usage into a monthly dollar cost.

Example

If your rate is $0.14 per kWh and an appliance uses 100 kWh per month, the monthly cost would be $14.00.
